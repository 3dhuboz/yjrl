# Nathan’s website checklist

We’re getting the club website ready for **2027**. Add what you know now; anything undecided can stay **Still coming**.

**Use the private checklist link supplied with this guide.** It opens only the checklist, so you won’t need an admin account. Keep the link handy to return later.

[Checklist start page](https://yjrl.pages.dev/website-checklist) · Club admins: **Admin → Website checklist**

1. **Sign-ups:** dates, age groups, fees and registration link.
2. **Contacts:** registrar, enquiries and website testers.
3. **Teams and games:** coaches, training times, fixture draw and ground locations.
4. **News and events:** stories, dates, details and photos.
5. **Uniforms and merchandise:** products, prices, photos, sizes and stock counts.
6. **Payments and collection:** payment options, pickup details and shop rules.
7. **Club photos and sponsors:** logos, pictures, captions and sponsor links.
8. **Final checks:** approved staff, safety contact and who will review the wording.

For each item, add notes or photos, then choose **Save for later** or **Ready for review**. You can come back and change things. Submitted content is checked before it goes onto the public website.

For photos, tell us what they show and where they should go. Only supply photos you have permission to share; photos of children need the club’s consent checks before publication. JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each.

Leave passwords, payment credentials and player medical information out of the checklist.

The detailed admin test steps are kept separately in [Admin testing reference](ops/ADMIN_TESTING_REFERENCE.md).
